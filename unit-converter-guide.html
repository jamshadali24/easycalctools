What Is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter changes a measurement from one unit to another. This can be useful for distance, weight and length calculations.

Available Conversions

  • Kilometers to miles
  • Miles to kilometers
  • Kilograms to pounds
  • Pounds to kilograms
  • Centimeters to inches
  • Inches to centimeters

How to Use It

  1. Enter the number you want to convert.
  2. Select the conversion type.
  3. Press Convert.
  4. Your result will appear immediately.
