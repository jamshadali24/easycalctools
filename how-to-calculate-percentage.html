How to Calculate Percentage

A simple percentage guide

What Is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a part of 100. Percentages are commonly used for discounts, marks, statistics and financial calculations.

Percentage Formula

To find what percentage one number is of another number:

Percentage = (Value ÷ Total) × 100

Example

If you scored 75 marks out of 100, your percentage is:

(75 ÷ 100) × 100 = 75%
