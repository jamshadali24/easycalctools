How to Calculate Your Age

A simple guide from EasyCalcTools

What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator determines how much time has passed between your date of birth and a selected date. The result is commonly expressed in years, months and days.

How Does Age Calculation Work?

To calculate your age, you need two dates: your date of birth and the current date. The calculator compares these dates and calculates the difference between them.

How to Use Our Age Calculator

  1. Open the Age Calculator on EasyCalcTools.
  2. Select your date of birth.
  3. Click the Calculate Age button.
  4. Your calculated age will appear on the screen.

Why Calculate Your Age?

Knowing your age can be useful when filling out forms, checking eligibility requirements, preparing applications, or simply finding out how old you are on a particular date.
