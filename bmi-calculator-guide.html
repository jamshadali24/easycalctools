Understand the basic BMI calculation

What Is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a calculation based on a person's weight and height.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²

How to Use Our Calculator

  1. Enter your weight in kilograms.
  2. Enter your height in centimeters.
  3. Click Calculate BMI.
  4. The calculator will display the calculated BMI.

Important Health Note

BMI is a general screening measurement and does not by itself provide a medical diagnosis. If you have health concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
