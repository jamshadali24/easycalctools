How to Calculate a Discount

Simple discount calculation guide

What Is a Discount?

A discount reduces the original price of a product or service by a specified percentage.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

Example

If an item costs $100 and has a 20% discount:

Discount = $100 × 20 ÷ 100 = $20

Final price = $100 − $20 = $80
